Question 1 A weather balloon rises higher into the atmosphere, moving farther from Earth's surface. What happens to the air pressure surrounding the balloon?

Detailed Explanation

Question 2 A diver notices that a balloon shrinks slightly underwater but a bottle of water stays nearly the same size. Which statement explains this observation?

Detailed Explanation

A (Correct): Gases have vast spaces between particles and compress readily under hydrostatic pressure. Liquid molecules are tightly packed, making liquids virtually incompressible.
B: Inverted claim; liquids resist compression far more strongly than gases.
C: Gases and liquids have completely different intermolecular separations and compressibilities.
D: Their volumetric responses under hydrostatic pressure are vastly unequal.

Question 4 In a hydraulic lift, the output piston has an area five times greater than the input piston. If the input force stays the same, what happens to the output force?

Detailed Explanation

A: Forces remain identical only if the piston surface areas are equal.
B (Correct): By Pascal's principle, pressure is uniform throughout ($P = F_1/A_1 = F_2/A_2$). Because the output area is 5× larger, the output force is 5× greater ($F_2 = 5 \times F_1$).
C: An output area that is larger multiplies force; it does not reduce it.
D: The mechanical advantage matches the area ratio of 5:1, not 2:1.
Question 5 The Moon exerts a gravitational pull on Earth's oceans, causing tides. Which change would increase the gravitational force between the Moon and Earth?

Detailed Explanation

A: Gravitational force is inversely proportional to distance squared; moving bodies farther apart weakens gravity.
B (Correct): Under Newton's law ($F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$), gravitational force is directly proportional to mass. Increasing the Moon's mass directly increases gravitational attraction.
C: Reducing mass decreases gravitational pull.
D: Reducing both masses significantly weakens the attraction.

Question 7 A rubber duck placed in a bathtub floats steadily on the surface of the water. How does Archimedes' principle explain why it doesn't sink?

Detailed Explanation

A (Correct): Archimedes' principle states that upward buoyant force equals the weight of displaced water. The duck floats because this upward buoyant force matches its downward gravitational weight.
B: Gravitational pull is always directed downward; weight cannot become negative.
C: Gravity acts on the duck's mass continuously regardless of surrounding fluid.
D: Buoyancy provides an external supporting force; it does not change the duck's physical mass.
Question 8 Inside a chimney, smoke and heated gases move upward and escape through the top. Which property of the heated gases explains this upward movement?

Detailed Explanation

A: Heating causes thermal expansion, which lowers density rather than increasing it.
B: Clinging to walls would impede upward draft rather than assist it.
C (Correct): When heated, gas molecules speed up and spread apart, lowering their density. Denser surrounding cool air sinks and forces the warm, less dense smoke upward (natural convection).
D: Gases expand when heated, creating an upward buoyant drive rather than sinking.

C: Gravitational pull acts vertically downward and is unaffected by rotation.
D: The trajectory curve is driven by aerodynamic fluid pressure, not thermodynamic conversion.
